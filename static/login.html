How it works

AI-powered HR intelligence, end to end.

VooVr transforms employee interactions, records and behavioral signals into intelligent workforce insights for modern HR teams.

01

Employee Profile

Every employee has a centralized profile with personal details, department, role, documents, meeting history and performance records.

02

Continuous Employee Insights

Meetings, feedback, attendance, interactions and HR activities continuously enrich each employee's profile with real-time data.

03

AI Workforce Intelligence

AI analyzes satisfaction, engagement, behavioral patterns and workplace signals to identify trends before they become problems.

04

Attrition & Risk Detection

VooVr identifies employees who may be at risk of disengagement or resignation by analyzing multiple workplace indicators.

05

Smart Follow-ups

AI recommends personalized HR actions and automatically schedules Gmail and WhatsApp follow-ups to maintain engagement.

06

Smarter HR Decisions

HR teams receive actionable insights, psychological analysis, workforce trends and employee recommendations from a single unified platform.

Why VooVr

Built for how HR actually works.

Security by design.

VooVr is built on enterprise-grade infrastructure with end-to-end encryption, strict access controls and comprehensive audit trails. Employee data is protected at every layer of the platform.

AES-256 protected records

Every employee record is encrypted using AES-256 standards at rest and in transit. Your data remains yours, secured by bank-grade cryptography.

Role-based access control

Granular permissions ensure HR admins, managers and executives see only what they need. No overexposure, no blind spots.

Audit logs & activity tracking

Every view, edit and action is logged with timestamps and identity markers. Full transparency for compliance and internal reviews.

Privacy-first HR platform

Employee trust is non-negotiable. VooVr is designed with data minimization principles, giving employees visibility into their own information and how it is used.
