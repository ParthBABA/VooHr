How speaker turns work Pick who speaks; every turn is labelled for you

The flow

  1. Choose who started the conversation.
  2. Type what they said in the transcript box.
  3. Press the ✓ tick to finish the turn and choose who speaks next.
  4. Repeat for every turn. Each finished turn shows its speaker’s name.

Good to know

No labels needed —
names are added automatically when you save or copy.
Recordings —
audio is added as one turn for the speaker you picked. Press the tick to split it into more turns.
Already labelled text —
upload or paste it and the existing speaker labels are converted into turns.

By how you add it

Paste or .txt —
drop in plain text (.txt).
WhatsApp chat —
in WhatsApp choose Export chat → Without media and upload the .txt.
Image or photo —
the picture is read word for word; check the extracted text, then press the tick between speakers.
Audio —
record or upload, then press the tick whenever the speaker changes.
