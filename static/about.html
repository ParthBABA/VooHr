What VooVr Does

VooVr is an AI operating system built for modern HR teams. We connect the entire employee lifecycle in one place — from the meeting to the record to the insight.

Managers use VooVr to track meetings, capture dictation and follow-ups, and surface employee risk-drift signals before small issues become big problems. Behind the scenes, our AI monitors satisfaction, engagement and behavioural trends so HR understands people, not just paperwork.
