What VooVr Does

VooVr is a privacy-first conversation memory for HR teams. It keeps meetings, voice notes and follow-ups in one place, so the context behind a decision is never lost between calls.

Managers dictate a note after a conversation and VooVr turns it into a record with action items and a suggested follow-up. HR reads what it produces, edits it, and decides what happens next — the software never acts on its own, and it never makes a judgement about a person.

Values

People first. Every feature starts with a real employee or manager need — never the other way around.

Privacy by default. We send the narrowest possible slice of data to each provider, encrypt selected personal fields at rest, keep role-based access controls and audit trails, and publish exactly who we share data with on our sub-processors page.

Humans decide. The output is a draft, not a verdict. No automated decision is ever made about an employee, and every suggestion is meant to be read, edited and owned by a person.
