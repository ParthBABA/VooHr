Sub-processors

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Who these are

Some of the work VooVr does — turning speech into text, generating a draft summary, sending a verification code, encrypting a field — is handled by specialist companies rather than by us. We call them sub-processors.

Each one processes data only to perform the specific job we have configured, on our instructions. We do not give our sub-processors permission to use your data for their own purposes, and we do not sell it.

Everything below is in our current codebase. “Default” means the provider used when you have not configured an alternative. “Optional” means you have to turn it on before it receives anything.

What conversation analysis receives

When a meeting transcript or voice note is analysed, the text of that conversation is sent to the configured language model provider so it can produce a summary, action items and follow-up drafts.

For a normal analysis request, the text sent does not include names, email addresses, phone numbers, employee IDs, department labels, IP addresses, document contents or any credentials.

Two things worth knowing: if you have a meeting recording, the audio file is sent to the transcription provider to be turned into text. And the periodic drift check is a separate request that also includes earlier derived scores and session dates, so the model can compare them over time.

Storage and encryption providers

Application records are stored in MongoDB. Selected personal fields are encrypted at the field level with AES-256-GCM using per-record keys that are wrapped with Google Cloud KMS. Transcripts and audio files are held in the database and in local file storage; they are protected by transport encryption and by access controls, but they are not individually field-encrypted.

International transfers

Several of the providers above process data outside your country of residence, including DeepSeek for conversation analysis. Where data is transferred abroad, it is transferred under the provider's own contractual terms and data-protection commitments. We keep the scope of what is shared narrow so that less data leaves your environment, and we do not share fields that are not needed for the task at hand.

Changes to this list

We review this list whenever we add, remove or change a provider. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page shows when it last changed. If you have questions about a provider listed here, contact us and we will explain what is sent and why.
